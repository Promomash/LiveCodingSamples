Database Schema

invoices— invoices issued to a customer
ColumnTypeDescription
idbigint, PKInvoice identifier.
customer_idbigint, FKCustomer the invoice was issued to.

payments— payments received from a customer
ColumnTypeDescription
idbigint, PKPayment identifier.

invoice_payments— allocation of payments to invoices
ColumnTypeDescription
idbigint, PKAllocation row identifier.
payment_idbigint, FKPayment the money comes from → payments.id.
invoice_idbigint, FKInvoice the money is applied to → invoices.id.
amountnumericAmount applied to that invoice.
deductions— deductions taken against invoices
ColumnTypeDescription
idbigint, PKDeduction identifier.
invoice_idbigint, FKInvoice the deduction was taken against → invoices.id.
reason_codetextDeduction reason code.
amountnumericDeduction amount.
created_attimestampDate and time the record was created.
statustextDeduction status.
